# Source corpus terms and public release scope

No source corpus images, transcriptions, raw OCR, candidate text, fitted vocabularies or model
weights are redistributed. FUNSD is recorded as research-use with redistribution prohibited.
OCR-D gt_structure_text is recorded as CC BY-SA 4.0 in manifests/licenses/registry.yaml;
its earlier unclear designation in the platform documentation is superseded. Per-volume
publication rights still require author confirmation. This release avoids corpus redistribution
under either designation. CORD and GT4HistOCR are recorded as CC BY 4.0 but are not released here.
Corpus and external-model terms are not overridden by the software MIT licence.

Obtain FUNSD at https://guillaumejaume.github.io/FUNSD/ and OCR-D at
https://github.com/OCR-D/gt_structure_text. The source download scripts/manifests are supplied;
frozen OCR/generator/context hashes must also match before any corpus-dependent rebuild.
CC BY 4.0 for authored supplementary materials remains pending author rights review.
